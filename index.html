What about freshwater?

EPA’s freshwater aquatic-life criteria concern dissolved chloride in water, not salt spread on roads: 860 mg/L as a one-hour average (acute) and 230 mg/L as a four-day average (chronic), each not exceeded more than once every three years on average. These national recommendations address chloride associated with sodium; they are not universal safety guarantees.

The effect of this scenario on Stony Brook cannot be calculated with the existing case-study data; runoff paths and chloride concentrations are not modeled.
